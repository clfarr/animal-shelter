Why This Matters in Trinity County

Trinity County's rural landscape means pets can wander miles from home. Dogs chasing deer, cats exploring, or animals spooked by wildlife can end up far from their families. Our Lost & Found program helps bridge that gap.

What happens to found animals:

  • We scan for microchips immediately
  • We check for ID tags and collars
  • We hold animals for the legally required period
  • We post found animals on our Petfinder and social media
  • We work hard to contact owners if we can identify them
  • After the hold period, unclaimed animals become available for adoption

Our Story 📖

Serving Trinity County and the surrounding Northern California communities, we're dedicated to caring for homeless and abandoned animals in our beautiful mountain region.

Our Mission

The Trinity County Animal Shelter provides a safe haven for lost, abandoned, and surrendered animals. We work tirelessly to reunite pets with their owners and find loving forever homes for those in need.

Every animal in our care receives proper nutrition, medical attention, and the kindness they deserve while waiting for their next chapter.

About Trinity County

Located in the heart of Northern California's Trinity Alps, our shelter serves one of the state's most rural and beautiful counties. Trinity County is known for its:

  • Stunning mountain scenery
  • Close-knit community
  • Outdoor lifestyle
  • Love for animals and nature
  • Strong community support

We're grateful for our community's continued support in helping animals find their way home.

Services We Provide

  • Animal shelter and care
  • Adoption services
  • Lost and found pet assistance
  • Community education
  • Working with rescue organizations
