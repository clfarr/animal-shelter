Our Story 📖

Founded in 1987 by local farmers who saw too many strays suffering, Hope Haven started in a converted barn. Today, we're still in that same barn (with some improvements!), still run mostly by volunteers, still doing everything we can for every animal that comes through our doors.

Our Promise

We may be small, but every animal here gets individual attention, medical care, and all the love we can give. We don't euthanize for space or time - once an animal comes to Hope Haven, we're in it together until they find their forever home.

We know every animal by name. We know their quirks, their favorite treats, their stories. And we can't wait to match them with families who'll love them as much as we do.

The Reality

Hope County is one of the poorest counties in the state. We don't have a big budget or fancy facilities. What we have is:

  • A community that cares
  • Volunteers who show up rain or shine
  • A local vet who gives us a discount
  • Neighbors who donate what they can
  • Hearts bigger than our bank account

Some months are harder than others. But we've never turned away an animal in need, and we don't plan to start.
